# RANK: ZERO

**Tagline:** Start at the Bottom. Rise Above Everyone.

RANK: ZERO is an online anime academy-life RPG concept focused on exploration, social strategy, school activities, competitive progression, and a changing academy hierarchy.

> **Project status:** Phase 1 — Architecture and design. No playable game systems have been implemented yet. Technology choices in the architecture documents are proposals until the Phase 1 review is approved.

## Project principles

- A standalone video-game experience, never a website-style dashboard.
- Hybrid 2D/2.5D presentation with isometric campus exploration and cinematic story scenes.
- Online multiplayer as a core requirement.
- Real players and simulated academy NPCs share the world.
- Four academy classes: D, C, B, and A. Promotion is earned and demotion is possible.
- A clean gameplay HUD; secondary systems live in an in-game side menu.
- Original characters, story, locations, art, and mechanics.
