# RANK: ZERO — Ranking and Class Progression

**Status:** Rules draft; formulas are not finalized.

## 1. Design goals

- Make competition meaningful and understandable.
- Reward consistent performance, not repetitive farming.
- Keep real-player rankings fair and server-verified.
- Track NPC standing separately from real-player leaderboards.
- Make promotion difficult but achievable.
- Allow demotion when sustained performance falls below class expectations.
- Avoid pay-to-win advantages.

## 2. Academy classes

| Class | Role | Progression expectation |
|---|---|---|
| D | Entry class | Learn systems and establish performance |
| C | Developing class | Demonstrate consistent performance |
| B | Advanced class | Maintain strong performance across activities |
| A | Elite class | Meet demanding standards and retain standing |

All new players begin in Class D. Promotion and demotion rules must be published and applied at defined review periods. A single unlucky match should not automatically demote a player.

## 3. Separate ranking tracks

Recommended tracks:
- Overall real-player academy ranking
- Class-specific real-player ranking
- Academic performance
- Competition performance
- Club/team contribution
- Seasonal ranking

NPC rankings are shown separately and clearly labelled as simulated academy standings. NPC results must not be mixed into the real-player leaderboard.

## 4. Scoring principles

Candidate scoring dimensions:
- Verified competition outcomes
- Academic activity and assessment results
- Mission objectives
- Team contribution
- Attendance/participation where appropriate
- Sportsmanship and rule compliance

The exact weights are open. Do not reward raw activity time alone. Use diminishing returns, daily/weekly caps where justified, duplicate-action detection, and anti-collusion checks.

## 5. Promotion and demotion review

Proposed review process:
1. Collect eligible performance over a defined review window.
2. Exclude invalidated or suspicious results.
3. Calculate the published class score.
4. Apply minimum participation/eligibility rules.
5. Promote players who meet the next-class threshold and available class capacity rules.
6. Retain players who meet current-class standards.
7. Place at-risk players into a transparent review/notice period before demotion, except for serious rule violations.
8. Record the decision and show the player a breakdown.

Class capacity and promotion quotas are not yet decided. Avoid hidden arbitrary quotas.

## 6. Competition integrity

- Server validates match rules and results.
- Client-submitted scores are untrusted.
- Each result has a unique event/match identifier.
- Duplicate rewards are rejected.
- Suspicious patterns are flagged for review.
- Ranking adjustments are auditable.
- Appeals and correction processes should exist.

## 7. Seasons and permanence

Potential model:
- Seasonal competitive ratings can reset or partially decay.
- Class standing can be reviewed by academy term.
- Permanent achievements, unlocked cosmetics, and history remain.
- Seasonal rewards should be cosmetic or convenience-oriented, never exclusive power boosts.

Reset cadence and reward schedule remain open.

## 8. Anti-farming and fairness

- Daily/weekly reward caps where appropriate.
- Diminishing returns for repeated identical tasks.
- Matchmaking by relevant skill band.
- No paid ranking points.
- No paid stat boosts that affect competitive outcomes.
- Detect repeated collusion and win-trading.
- Keep ranking calculations server-side.

## 9. Open decisions

- Scoring formula and weights
- Review window duration
- Promotion thresholds and class capacity
- Demotion warning/appeal process
- Seasonal duration and reset behavior
- Matchmaking rating model
- Public visibility and privacy settings
